Pilot terms

The kit is free. In return, we'd love your before and after photos and a few answers, but you don't have to give them.

Who can take part

You need to be 18 or over and live in the UK, with mould on a wall, ceiling or window frame at home. There's one kit per request, and each kit cleans a patch about the size of two A4 sheets. We have 1,000 kits, so we may stop taking requests without notice.

Using the kit

  • Follow the instructions in the app and on the pouch.
  • Keep the powder away from your eyes.
  • Don't use it on freshly painted walls.

What Urrgh does and doesn't do

Urrgh cleans mould stains. It doesn't fix the cause of damp, so mould can come back. That's why we check in after 30 days. If you rent, report damp and mould to your landlord in writing, whatever the result. Using a kit doesn't affect your landlord's responsibilities.

Your photos and answers

You keep ownership of your photos. By uploading them, you let us use them and your answers to test, improve and promote Urrgh, including for research and training software. We only publish photos that can't identify you or your home. See our privacy notice.
