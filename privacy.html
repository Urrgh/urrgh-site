Every 3 years we review whether we still need the photos and answers, and delete what we don't. Your phone removes location data from photos before they're sent, and we strip any that's left. Please keep people, faces and documents out of shot.

Our legal basis is our legitimate interest in running the pilot and improving our product. For your address, it's posting the kit you asked for. For your email, it's your consent. To withdraw it, reply to any of our emails or write to privacy@urrgh.com, and we'll delete your address.

Who else sees it

We don't sell your data or share your record with your landlord or letting agent. You can download your record and share it yourself. We use these services to run the pilot:

  • Cloudflare runs the app and stores the data. Photos are stored in its EU region.
  • Resend sends our emails from its EU (Ireland) region.
  • Google Workspace handles emails you send us.
  • Royal Mail delivers your kit, so it gets your name and address.

Where a service is based outside the UK, it is covered by UK-approved safeguards.

We may publish results and photos, including in our marketing. We only use photos that show no people and nothing that could identify you or your home.

Cookies

We use two cookies, both needed for the app to work. One remembers that this phone started your kit, and the other keeps you signed in after you open your email link. Both last up to a year. We don't use analytics or advertising cookies.

Your rights

You can ask us for a copy of your data, or to correct or delete it, and you can object to how we use it. Email privacy@urrgh.com with your kit code, and we'll reply within a month. If you're unhappy with our answer, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint or on 0303 123 1113.
